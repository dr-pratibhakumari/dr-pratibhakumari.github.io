About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Bhopal.

Prior to joining IISER Bhopal, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher. I obtained my direct Ph.D. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, India, and my B.Tech. degree from the National Institute of Technology Hamirpur, India.

My research focuses on developing continual learning, online learning, and streaming machine learning solutions for applications involving evolving data. I am also interested in computer vision, medical image analysis, smart agriculture, and multimedia surveillance.
