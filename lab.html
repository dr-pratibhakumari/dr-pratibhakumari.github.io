IISER Bhopal Research Laboratory

ALIVE Lab

Adaptive Learning for Intelligent and Varying Environments

We develop adaptive machine learning methods for intelligent systems operating in dynamic and evolving environments, with applications across computer vision, healthcare, agriculture, and multimedia analytics.

What we work on

Current Focus

CP

Computational Pathology

Machine learning methods for analysing digitized pathology images and supporting data-driven clinical research.

CL

Continual Learning

Learning from changing data streams while retaining useful knowledge acquired from earlier tasks.

OL

Online & Streaming Learning

Adaptive models that update efficiently as new data arrives over time.

CV

Computer Vision

Visual learning methods for recognition, analysis, and interpretation of complex image and video data.

MI

Medical Image Analysis

Intelligent methods for extracting meaningful information from biomedical and clinical images.

SA

Smart Agriculture

Data-driven tools for monitoring, analysis, and decision support in agricultural environments.

MS

Multimedia Surveillance

Learning methods for understanding and monitoring complex multimedia and video streams.
