About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Data Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal, India.

Prior to joining IISER Bhopal, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher in Faculty of Informatics and Data Science (FIDS) at University of Regensburg, Germany. I obtained my direct Ph.D. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Ropar, India, and my B.Tech. degree from the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Hamirpur, India.

My broad research interests include continual learning, online and streaming learning, computer vision, medical image analysis, smart agriculture, and multimedia surveillance.
