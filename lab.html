ALIVE Lab

Adaptive Learning for Intelligent and Varying Environments

The ALIVE Lab develops adaptive machine learning methods for intelligent systems operating in dynamic and varying environments. Our work focuses on learning from evolving data, with applications in computer vision and interdisciplinary domains.

Current Focus

  • Continual learning
  • Online and streaming learning
  • Computer vision
  • Medical image analysis
  • Smart agriculture
  • Multimedia surveillance
